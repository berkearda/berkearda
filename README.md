## Research

**CroissantMiner: Automated Extraction and Validation of Croissant Metadata for ML Datasets**<br>
Berke Arda et al. (lead author). *NeurIPS 2026, Evaluations and Datasets track.*<br>
[Code](https://github.com/berkearda/croissantminer) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/croissantminer/croissantminer)

A benchmark for extracting Croissant metadata from ML dataset papers: 602 papers, 102 of them with gold annotations from 22 annotators. Across 24 extraction systems, single-pass extraction scores higher than four agentic architectures on every backbone, while the agentic variants cost 1.2 to 7.8 times as much per paper.

**SkillEval: skill-aware evaluation of LLMs** (first author, MSc thesis; under review)

Profiles 3,811 LLMs across 100 interpretable skills from 36 million responses. Routing questions by skill matches the strongest single LLM at 38% of its inference cost.
